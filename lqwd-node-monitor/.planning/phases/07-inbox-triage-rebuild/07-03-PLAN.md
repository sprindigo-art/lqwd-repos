| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|

| T-07-11 | Information disclosure | facet counts leaking post-filter (correctness) | mitigate | `facetCounts` computed over the unfiltered `view.cards`, independent of active facets (Pitfall 2) |
